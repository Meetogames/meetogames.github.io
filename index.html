About me

Hi! I'm Dmitry, or just Meeto

I make games on my own, and AI helps me with it: code, art, sound, testing. It lets one person do the work of a small team.

I've been playing games my whole life, since I was a kid, and they have always been my biggest passion. Now I want to make quality games that are genuinely interesting to play.

Some of my games are bright and cozy, others are dark and scary. What they share is attention to how they feel in your hands.
