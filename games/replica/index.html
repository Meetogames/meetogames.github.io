ABOUT

From two to six players wake up in different corners of a bunker that is built anew every round. It is pitch dark: you only see what you can reach with your hand. Find a key, open one of the blast doors and get out.

Only one player can pass through an open door, and the fewer of you escape, the more points each one gets. On the second map, a snowy town, you have to reach a shelter before the strike.

No interface during the round: only sound, light and the voices of other players. The menu is an old terminal in a bunker room with a window onto a giant panopticon.

HOW IT WORKS

2–6 players online

Play with friends or strangers, with voice chat.

A new bunker every round

The map is built from scratch and grows with the number of players.

Pitch darkness

You only see what is within reach. Your eyes slowly get used to the dark.

One way out per door

Four blast doors, and only one player gets through each of them.

Sound gives you away

Running and heavy breathing can be heard. Sneak, hide under a bed, stay quiet.

PS1 look and a terminal menu

Low-poly models, pixel textures and an old monitor instead of menus.
