Jelly Swirl

A bright candy marble shooter with 300 levels. Pop the candy chains before they roll into the swirl.

Phone and computer7 languagesMarble shooter
Play free

About the game

A cheerful jelly sits in the middle of a sugar spiral and shoots candies at the chains rolling toward the swirl. Match three or more of the same color to pop them. When the gap closes on the same color, the chain snaps back and sets off a combo.

Every chapter brings a new candy world, new goals and new kinds of candies. Use bombs, rainbows, slow motion and rewind when things get tight, open star chests and come back for the daily gift.

300 levels, 15 chapters

Every chapter has its own candy world: strawberry, mint, caramel sunset, blueberry, lime and more.

A goal in every level

Clear the spiral, collect star candies, pop candies of one color or chain combos.

New candies along the way

Frozen candies, rock candies and chocolate bridges keep every chapter fresh.

Four boosters

Candy bomb, rainbow, slow motion and rewind for the trickiest levels.

Daily gift and star chest

The gift grows every day in a row, and every 12 new stars open a chest.

Leaderboard

Your best scores on all levels add up. See how you rank against other players.

How to play

Mouse and keyboard

  • Aim with the mouse and click to shoot
  • Right-click or Space swaps the candies
  • Keys 1-4 use boosters
  • P pauses the game

Phone and tablet

  • Drag to aim and let go to shoot
  • Tap the jelly to swap the candies
  • Tap a booster to use it

It's free and runs right in your browser.
