About the game

A cozy 3D .io game about a sticky ball. Roll around a low-poly town and pick up everything smaller than you: crumbs, buttons and paperclips first, then bikes, benches, trees, cars, buses and finally whole houses.

Nine rivals roll the same map, and a bigger ball rolls up a smaller one. When two balls of a similar size meet, they lock in a tug-of-war: tap fast to push or break free.

From crumbs to houses

Start smaller than a coin and finish the round bigger than a house.

Nine rivals

Roll up smaller balls and keep away from the bigger ones.

Tug-of-war clinches

When two balls of a similar size meet, tap faster than your rival.

10-minute rounds

The biggest ball wins when the timer runs out.

14 skins and 11 trails

Make your ball look the way you like.

Upgrades and a daily gift

Spend coins on speed, start size and a magnet.

How to play

Mouse and keyboard

  • Move the mouse or use WASD / arrow keys to roll
  • Space or click to tap in a clinch
  • P to pause

Phone and tablet

  • Drag your finger to roll
  • Tap the screen in a clinch
