About the game

Pogost is a fast first-person shooter on arenas. Waves of enemies come one after another, every level gives you experience and an upgrade card, and between runs you spend what you earned on permanent upgrades.

The world is made of toys, but the story behind it is serious: it is about the fight for life. Little Mito and the gothic girl Mina are each fighting their own battle, and the arenas are where it happens.

The game is in development on Unity. The screenshots show an early build.

Waves and upgrade cards

Survive waves of enemies, level up and pick an upgrade card every level.

Fast movement

Triple jump, a dash that makes you untouchable and a ground slam with a shockwave.

Weapons with weight

Recoil, a sliding bolt and physical shell casings with their own sound.

Toy enemies that break

Plastic soldiers lose helmets and arms, and the pieces stay on the arena.

Own soundtrack

The lights and the interface pulse to the beat of the music.

Progress between runs

Spend what you earn on permanent upgrades and come back stronger.
